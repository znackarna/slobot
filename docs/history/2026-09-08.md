### 2026-09-08 — The audio row could not succeed for a video, and had nowhere to say so

The owner ticked `Zvuk` and got *Do této přípony zvuk uložit neumíme. Vyberte
MP3, M4A nebo WAV.* — advice with nowhere to follow it, since the file name is
settled in the system's own dialog.

**And it was worse than a missing control.** `nameFor` built the audio's name
from the source's extension, whatever it was; `folders.rs` names a codec for
`mp3`, `m4a`, `aac` and `wav` and refuses the rest. So a video source proposed
`nahravka.mp4`, which is a container the core cannot write — **for every video
in the archive that row could not succeed at all.** His has one recording and it
is an `.mp4`.

His suggestion, taken as it was: three small buttons on the row itself. MP3,
M4A, WAV, and the row opens on the source's own where the core can write it, so
an `.m4a` recording is still copied rather than re-encoded — which is what the
row promised before there was anything to choose. Anything else opens on MP3.

**What was weighed and not done.** A fourth button carrying the source's own
container would keep the copy for `.opus`, `.flac`, `.ogg` and `.wma` sources
too, which `AUDIO_ONLY_FORMATS` copies through today. The archive holds none of
those and the online import writes `m4a`, so the fourth button would pay in a
harder row for a case that does not arise. Recorded rather than assumed: the
extensions were counted in the archive first.

The buttons sit against the right edge, across both text lines rather than
under them, so the row keeps the height it had — his correction, and the right
one: a third line made the audio row taller than the five beside it for a
choice that is not a third fact about it. 26 px against the segmented control's
30, because that one sits in a track and this one stands in a list.

**And the third column broke the other five rows on the way.** A grid fills the
first free cell, so with a column standing open at the right, the note of every
row without buttons went into it — sitting beside its own name instead of under
it. Only the audio row looked right, because its buttons had taken that cell.
Both texts are told which column they are in now. Measured rather than looked
at: three rows, all 59.75 px, notes and names on the same left edge, buttons
14 px from the right — the row's own padding.

`Původní nahrávka` became `Samotný zvuk`: once a container can be chosen, the
old line is a promise the row no longer keeps — WAV out of an MP3 is not the
original. His copy, so it is a proposal.

Verified: four new cases in `saveRecording.test.ts`, and putting the old naming
back fails all four. 329 frontend tests.

### 2026-09-08 — What the application says goes where the front is

Saving from the language model's dialog put the notice bar across it. The bar
carries `z-index: 70` on purpose — that was the repair for messages raised
while a dialog was open going behind the scrim, and the reason the wizard
stopped drawing a panel of its own. What the repair left is what he saw: *cele
to pozadí je rozmazane, nad tim je dialog a pak tam zacne viset ta lista.* A
coloured strip on a blurred backdrop, belonging to nothing on screen and
painted over the dialog underneath.

**Two answers were weighed and the first one was wrong.** Mine was to push the
dialog down so the bar stops covering it — which keeps one place for messages
and is what I argued for. He said it would look ugly, and he was right: the bar
would still be floating on the blur, attached to nothing, just no longer
overlapping. Stopping an overlap is not the same as belonging somewhere.

His was to put the message in the dialog's own header. My objection to it was
that a dialog can only report what it raised itself — a download finishing or a
version found while a dialog is open belongs to neither — so it would leave two
places for messages and the overlapping bar would still be needed for the rest.

The answer is his with that objection removed: **one bar that goes where the
front is.** It is not the dialog reporting its own outcome; it is that a message
is said on whatever surface the reader is looking at. A dialog is open, so it is
said there — whatever raised it.

`useDialog` is where it is registered, because it is the one door every modal in
this application goes through: no dialog carries a slot, and there is still one
bar, one component and one rule. The list is in the order dialogs opened, so a
dialog opened over another takes the front from it.

`createPortal` appends, and the bar belongs above the content rather than below
the footer, so `NoticeBar` puts a host at the top of the dialog and portals into
that. `useSyncExternalStore` rather than an effect: the front has to be known
during the render that draws the bar, or a message raised in the same tick a
dialog opens is drawn on the page first and jumps.

Inside a dialog the bar drops its `z-index` — nothing there can paint over it,
and keeping it would lift it above a dialog opened on top of this one.

**Two corrections came from looking at it, and both were mine.**

The first was a bleed. The bar was pulled to the dialog's edges with
`margin: -24px -26px`, the padding `.dialog` carries — and the language model's
preview carries its own, which is nought. So the bar went out through the top
edge and the window clipped it. There are no negative margins now: the bar takes
whatever box it is put in.

The second was the place. The top of the dialog is above the heading, and that
dialog's heading is followed by a row of tabs the reader needs to keep seeing —
so the bar covered the way around the very screen it was reporting on. He drew
the line he wanted it under, and it is under the tabs, where the content starts.

Rather than deducing that from the markup, **the dialog says.** An empty
`.dialog-notice-slot` marks the spot; a dialog that says nothing still gets the
top. One element in one dialog, and no rule guessing at what a header is.

It is square and sits flush on that boundary — his correction again: inside a
dialog the bar is a band across it, not a card lying on it, and the rounded
corners over a straight edge were what made it read as a second thing on top of
the first.

`NoticeBar.test.tsx` is new: on the page with no dialog, inside one when there
is, at the top by default, where the dialog says when it says, said once rather
than twice, and back on the page when the dialog closes. Put the bar back on the
page and three of the six fail; ignore the slot and the sixth does. 335 frontend
tests.
